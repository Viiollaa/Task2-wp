01/DISCOVER

Where Tech Meets Creativity

MORE THAN JUST CODE

Hi, I'm Viola! I am a Computer Science undergraduate student who loves exploring logic alongside aesthetics. Beyond algorithms and syntax, I have a strong passion for designing visual spaces and clean web layouts.

What makes my journey unique is the balance between structure and art. Whether I'm working through code or painting on a fresh canvas, I bring curiosity, focus, and creativity into everything I do.

When I take a break from screen time, you will usually find me painting, experimenting with color palettes, or trying out cake decoration ideas. Art gives me a quiet space to express ideas through texture and tone.
